How the value is worked out Held-out tests

These protocols already have a token that trades, so the radar never ranks them. It learns from them instead: the model is fitted on every traded protocol in the snapshot with a recorded market cap and TVL — the largest of them are shown here at the market's own values — and each board row's launch odds are read from how many of its closest peers are on this side of the line. The two right-hand columns grade the model in the open: each peer's leave-one-out price is what the fitted model says it is worth with that peer left out of the fit, and the error is the factor between that price and the market's own.

Built from one sealed snapshot; the page runs with no network and every figure on it is in its data file.
